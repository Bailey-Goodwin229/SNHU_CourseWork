Briefly summarize the DriverPass project. Who was the client? What type of system did they want you to design?

DriverPass was a system that wanted to be created by a business man who saw an opportunity to fill a hole that is left in the market of people trying to get their driving license. He wanted the system to be for customers who would log on and be able to pay for driving packages, schedule times, have a personal profile with information, take practice tests and review the current guidlines by the DMV. They wanted the application to be over the internet so a wide range of users could access it and so that employees could use the system from wherever they wanted.
