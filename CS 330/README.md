This project involved the development of a complex 3D scene rendered in real-time, based on a 2D reference image. Using C++ and the OpenGL graphics API, I recreated a collection of real-world objects by identifying their underlying basic shapes and applying advanced rendering techniques. 

How do I approach designing software?

When designing software, I try to break a complex problem into manageable parts. For this scene, that meant breaking the reference image into a series of shapes that I worked on one at a time through a series of stages. This allowed me to give each shape the proper attention it deserved, leading to a polished finished product.

What new design skills has your work on the project helped you to craft?

This project has given me new knowledge on working with spatial techniques and coordinate transformations. I learned how to place shapes in their appropriate positions to mirror real life as closely as possible. This involved working with multiple objects in a shared scene, which required careful planning and the constant adjustment of values to get everything to fit together seamlessly.

What design process did you follow for your project work?

I used an incremental design process: rendering a shape, moving it into its correct position, applying a texture, and finally applying lighting. Refinement and changes were made continuously along the way to achieve the final look.

How could tactics from your design approach be applied in future work?

These tactics are the essence of software development. Creating reusable functions, solving problems one piece at a time, and breaking large problems into smaller ones are universal skills. Furthermore, making small changes followed by testing ensures that I can build a system without breaking existing functionality.

How do I approach developing programs?

The first part of developing a program is envisioning how it will work and defining its goal before writing any code. Without a clear goal, it is impossible to know what to build. Once the project is planned and broken down, the code is written incrementally and tested frequently. This ensures the program continues to work throughout the development lifecycle until it is complete and thoroughly vetted.

What new development strategies did you use while working on your 3D scene?

I relied heavily on technical documentation to understand how to apply textures and lighting effectively. I also wrote detailed comments to explain each section and the purpose of each function, which made it much easier to integrate new features into the scene as the project progressed.

How did iteration factor into your development?

The entire project was built using iteration. The workflow involved placing a shape, adjusting its transform, adding texture, and then applying lighting. I ensured each iteration was stable before moving to the next shape, which made the development process much easier to manage and follow.

How has your approach to developing code evolved throughout the milestones?

Each milestone functioned as an individual iteration. As I progressed, I learned the specific steps required to create a complete object, which I then scaled up to create the entire scene. My approach evolved from simply placing shapes to understanding the full pipeline—from geometry to texture to light—for each individual object, preventing the confusion that would have come from trying to do everything at once.
